Career Path Recommendation System

Discover the Career That Matches Your Skills.

Rate five core skills and we will compare your profile with eight sample career paths. The system uses cosine similarity — the same idea as matching two vectors — so your top matches change with the scores you enter. Use the results as a conversation starter, not as a final decision.

Discover My Career

How the system works

1

Rate your skills

Score programming, communication, creativity, mathematics, and problem solving from 1 to 10 using sliders.

2

We compare profiles

Your vector is compared with every career in the dataset using scikit-learn cosine similarity. Higher similarity means a closer skill shape.

3

See your top 3

Get the three closest careers, match percentages, important skills, and ideas for what to explore next.

Benefits of career recommendations

Find new options

Meet roles you may not have considered yet, from data science to project management.

Plan what to learn

Each result highlights important skills and practical next steps to study or practise.

Start conversations

Share your results with teachers, mentors, or advisors as a starting point.
